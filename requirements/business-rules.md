# Business Rules

## BRULE-001
A transfer recipient can be identified only if the provided phone number belongs to a client registered with the same bank.

## BRULE-002
The client must be informed of the result of every transfer, regardless of whether the transfer is successful or unsuccessful.

## BRULE-003
A client can access their transfer history for the previous 12 months.

## BRULE-004
A client can view only transfers in which they participated as either the sender or the recipient.

## BRULE-005
A client can view only their own account balance and cannot access the balance of another client.

## BRULE-006
A client can view information about their current transfer limit but cannot modify the limit themselves.
