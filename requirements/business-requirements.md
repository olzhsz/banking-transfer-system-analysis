Business problems:
1. Customers cannot independently find other customers within the banking app.
2. Customers cannot get notified about money transfers without logging into the app.
3. Customers have a monthly transfer limit but cannot see how much of that limit remains.
4. Customers cannot independently check their transfer history within the app.

Business goals:
1. Make finding the recipient more convenient and reduce reliance on bank details.
2. Ensure that clients are promptly informed about the result of their transfers.
3. Make the limit status transparent to the client.
4. Provide the client with the ability to view their transfer history independently.

Business Requirements:
1. Clients must have an alternative way to identify a transfer recipient without using card details.
2. The client must receive information about the result of the transfer in a timely manner.
3. The client must receive up-to-date information regarding transfer limits.
4. Each client must be able to independently access information about their previous transfers.
