# Business Requirements

## Business Problems

### BP-001
Customers cannot independently find other customers within the banking app.

### BP-002
Customers cannot get notified about money transfers without logging into the app.

### BP-003
Customers have a monthly transfer limit but cannot see how much of that limit remains.

### BP-004
Customers cannot independently check their transfer history within the app.

---

## Business Goals

### BG-001
Make finding the recipient more convenient and reduce reliance on bank details.

### BG-002
Ensure that clients are promptly informed about the result of their transfers.

### BG-003
Make the limit status transparent to the client.

### BG-004
Provide the client with the ability to view their transfer history independently.

---

## Business Requirements

### BR-001
Clients must have an alternative way to identify a transfer recipient without using card details.

### BR-002
The client must receive information about the result of the transfer in a timely manner.

### BR-003
The client must receive up-to-date information regarding transfer limits.

### BR-004
Each client must be able to independently access information about their previous transfers.
